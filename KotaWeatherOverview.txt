Overview of Kota:
Kota, Rajasthan, is a city known for its historical significance and educational prominence. Founded in the 13th century, Kota developed into an industrial hub by the 20th century. Geographically, it is located in the southeast of Rajasthan, along the Chambal River, providing fertile land for agriculture. Culturally, Kota is famous for its traditional crafts, vibrant festivals, and educational institutions, including coaching centers that cater to competitive exams. The city is also known for landmarks such as the Kota Garh Palace and the Chambal Garden, attracting tourists and contributing to its economic growth.
